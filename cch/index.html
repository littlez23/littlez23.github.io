  • Who shows up when a kid changes schools mid-semester? Not the old teacher, not the old coach. Usually the mentor, and behind the mentor is a small staff somebody has to pay for.
  • Would you rather donate and wonder, or buy a specific thing and know? We sell specific things. One placement, one cycle, one kid, one advocate, itemized.
  • We do not do galas. The money goes to background checks, training hours, mileage, and one full-time coordinator per region. That is a plain budget and we are happy to show it to anyone who asks.
  • I want to be honest about the part that is boring. Most of what we fund is paperwork, mileage reimbursement, and a staff person who returns phone calls. The dramatic part happens because the boring part got paid for.
  • Twelve dollars covers the background check on a volunteer who will stay in a kid's life for two years. That is the whole math of the thing at its smallest unit. If you want to fund something you can picture, that is the picture.
  • Trust takes months, and months cost money. That is the least inspiring sentence in this message and the truest one. If you would rather fund duration than a single event, we are the right place to send it.
  • The gap is not good intentions, it is payroll and mileage. We have adults willing to mentor and kids who need them, and a bottleneck made of ordinary expenses. Clearing part of that bottleneck is what your contribution buys.
  • It is fair to want proof before you spend, and we are not offended by the question. Ask us for our screening process, our staff list, and where last year's money went. Then decide whether to buy a match.
  • Half of this work is paperwork and I will not pretend otherwise. Screening, references, training records, case notes. Donors who understand that are the ones who keep us solvent, and we would like to add you to that list.
  • One boy asked his mentor to teach him how to shake hands before a job interview. They practiced in a parking lot for ten minutes. Somebody paid for that pairing to exist, and the slot for the next kid is unpaid right now.
